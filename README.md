Hi, I'm Partha. I build software and work with data.

I started on the design side. For a year I was a UI/UX designer at Druooz in Bangalore, where I designed their cab booking platform: the rider app, the driver app, the website, and the ERP their operations team runs on. Working that closely with the developers who built those screens is what pulled me toward engineering.

These days I mostly write Python and Java, and I'm spending more and more of my time on data science. The design background still shows up in how I build. I care about how a thing feels to use.
